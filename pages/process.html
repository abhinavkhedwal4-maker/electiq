Step by Step

How Elections Work

A complete visual guide through every phase of a democratic election — from announcement to final results.

1
Pre-Election

📣 Election Announcement

The Election Commission officially announces the election schedule, including dates for nominations, voting, and counting. This triggers the Model Code of Conduct (MCC) — a set of rules that all political parties and candidates must follow during the campaign period.

Key Facts:
  • In India, the Election Commission of India (ECI) announces schedules
  • MCC comes into force immediately upon announcement
  • Government cannot announce new welfare schemes during this period
⏱️ Typically 4–6 weeks before voting
2
Pre-Election

📝 Voter Registration & Roll Updates

Citizens must be registered on the Electoral Roll to vote. Authorities update voter lists, remove deceased voters, add new eligible voters (age 18+), and allow corrections of existing entries.

How to Register (India):
  • Apply online via voters.eci.gov.in or Form 6
  • Provide proof of age and residence
  • Receive Voter ID Card (EPIC)
  • Rolls are published for public inspection
⏱️ Ongoing, with special drives before elections
3
Pre-Election

🙋 Nomination of Candidates

Candidates file their nomination papers within the specified window. Each nomination must be accompanied by a security deposit and signed by a certain number of registered voters (proposers). Nominations are then scrutinized for validity.

Requirements in India:
  • Security deposit: ₹25,000 for Lok Sabha, ₹10,000 for State Assemblies
  • Must be a citizen of India aged 25+ (Lok Sabha)
  • Cannot have criminal convictions of 2+ years
  • Must file criminal & financial affidavits
⏱️ 2–3 weeks before voting
4
Pre-Election

📢 Election Campaign

Candidates and parties conduct public rallies, door-to-door canvassing, advertisements, and debates to seek votes. Campaigns must follow strict expenditure limits and content rules set by the Election Commission.

Campaign Rules:
  • No campaigning 48 hours before polling (silence period)
  • Expense limits: ₹95 lakh per Lok Sabha candidate
  • Paid political ads must be pre-certified by MCC committee
  • Hate speech, bribery of voters is prohibited
⏱️ Weeks 1–3 of election period
5
Voting Day

🗳️ Polling / Voting Day

Eligible voters visit their designated polling booths and cast their votes. In India, votes are cast using Electronic Voting Machines (EVMs) which are tamper-proof. A VVPAT (Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail) slip is generated for verification.

Voting Process (India):
  • Bring valid photo ID (Voter ID, Aadhaar, Passport, etc.)
  • Name verified against electoral roll
  • Indelible ink applied to left index finger
  • Press candidate's button on EVM
  • VVPAT slip visible for 7 seconds for verification
⏱️ Polling hours: 7 AM – 6 PM generally
6
Post-Election

🔢 Vote Counting

On counting day, EVM units are brought to designated counting centres. Counting Agents from each candidate observe the count. Results are declared round by round, and the candidate with the highest votes (First Past The Post) wins.

How Counting Works:
  • Postal ballots counted first
  • EVMs from each polling station counted in rounds
  • Running tally displayed publicly and on ECI website
  • Returning Officer declares winner officially
⏱️ Begins at 8 AM on counting day
7
Post-Election

🏛️ Formation of Government

After results, the party or alliance with a majority forms the government. The leader is sworn in as Prime Minister / Chief Minister. The President or Governor plays a constitutional role in the transfer of power.

Key Steps:
  • Party/Alliance with 272+ seats (Lok Sabha) gets majority
  • President invites majority leader to form government
  • Cabinet is constituted and sworn in
  • New government presents Budget within 6 months
⏱️ Within 2–3 weeks of results
⚡

Understanding EVMs & VVPATs

What is an EVM?

An Electronic Voting Machine is a standalone, battery-operated device that records votes electronically. It has two units: a Control Unit (with officials) and a Ballot Unit (with voters).

What is VVPAT?

Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail is a machine connected to EVM that prints a paper slip showing the candidate and symbol voted for. The voter can verify it through a transparent window for 7 seconds.

Is EVM Hackable?

EVMs are standalone machines — not connected to internet or any network. They are programmed once and sealed. Supreme Court of India has upheld their reliability multiple times.

NOTA Option

Since 2013, voters can press NOTA (None Of The Above) if they disapprove of all candidates. This is a constitutional right under the right to expression.

Electoral Systems Worldwide

🇮🇳

India

First Past The Post (FPTP) system. Parliamentary Democracy. 543 Lok Sabha seats. Largest democracy by voters.

🇺🇸

USA

Electoral College system. Presidential Democracy. Each state has electors. 270 electoral votes needed to win.

🇬🇧

UK

First Past The Post. Westminster Parliamentary system. 650 constituencies. Paper ballot voting.

🇩🇪

Germany

Mixed-Member Proportional system. Two votes per voter — for candidate and party. Ensures proportional representation.
